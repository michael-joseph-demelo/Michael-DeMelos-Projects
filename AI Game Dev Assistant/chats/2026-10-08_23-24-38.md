## You

In two sentences: which keys or mouse buttons are punch_left and punch_right bound to?

## Assistant

In the Input Map in `project.godot`, `punch_left` is the left mouse button (`button_index` 1) and `punch_right` is the right mouse button (`button_index` 2). Each has only that one binding, and `CharacterBase.gd` → `handle_fist_inputs()` uses them to throw the matching fist toward the cursor.
