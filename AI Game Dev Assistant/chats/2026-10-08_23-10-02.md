# How the fist physics works

The fists don't use Godot's physics engine. A fist is a plain `Node2D`, and its position is set directly every physics frame by math in `fists/FistBase.gd`. The only physics-engine involvement is collision detection through the `Hitbox` (`Area2D`). Fists don't stop at walls or bodies. They only react through hitbox signals.

Each fist has three states, driven by the `punching` and `retracting` flags:

```
idle (parented to slot) → punch() → punching → _start_retraction() → retracting → _reset_fist() → idle
```

---

## 1. Launch: `punch()`

When you press `punch_left` or `punch_right`, `CharacterBase.handle_fist_inputs()` calls `fist.punch(get_global_mouse_position())`. The punch is ignored if the fist is already busy or disabled.

`punch()` then:
- Records `punch_origin`, `initial_direction`, and `aim_target`.
- **Detaches both the fist and its slot node** from the character and re-parents them to the scene root:
  ```gdscript
  original_slot_parent.remove_child(slot_node)
  original_fist_parent.remove_child(self)
  root.add_child(slot_node)
  root.add_child(self)
  ```
  As a result, the fist travels in world space. Moving or rotating the character mid-punch doesn't drag it along.
- Turns on hitbox monitoring (deferred) and sets `punching = true`.

The slot node also travels with the fist, because `slot_node.global_position = ...` is set every frame. This matters because the slot is the `end_point` of the `ChainDrawer`.

---

## 2. Travel: two movement modes

`_physics_process()` picks the mode based on `arc_strength`:

```gdscript
if arc_strength > 0.0:
    _process_bezier_punch(delta, spin_dir)
else:
    _process_direct_punch(delta, spin_dir, effective_speed)
```

**`arc_strength` defaults to `1.0`.** Bezier is therefore the default. A fist only uses direct mode if `FistStats.json` sets `arc_strength` to `0` (or a negative value) for that `fist_name`. I can't see the JSON, so I can't tell which mode each of your fists uses.

### A. Bezier arc: `_process_bezier_punch()`
- The fist follows a quadratic Bezier curve:
  - `p0` is the origin.
  - `p2` is `max_distance` straight along the initial aim direction.
  - `p1` is the midpoint pushed sideways by `arc_strength * 50`.
  - `curve_sign` makes left fists curve one way and right fists the other, like hooks.
- Progress is `punch_t += speed * delta / max_distance`. Punch **duration** is always `max_distance / speed`, no matter how curved the path is. Stronger arcs travel a longer path in the same time, so they move faster visually.
- Range is fixed: it always goes the full `max_distance`, even if the cursor was closer.
- **There is no cursor tracking.** `use_cursor_tracking` has no effect in this mode.
- Rotation faces the movement direction (`move_dir.angle() - PI / 2`).
- The punch ends when `punch_t >= 1.0`.

### B. Direct punch with steering: `_process_direct_punch()`
- The fist moves at constant `effective_speed` along `current_direction`.
- If `use_cursor_tracking` is on (player fists), the fist steers toward the live mouse position:
  - It measures how far the target is off to the side of the original line: `origin_to_target.dot(perp)`.
  - That distance is scaled to an influence of -1..1, saturating at 100 px.
  - `current_direction` is lerped toward the bent direction at `8.0 * delta`. This gives smooth curving, not snapping.
- Enemy fists have `use_cursor_tracking = false`, set in `NuggetDummy._init_fist()`. They steer toward the fixed `aim_target`, which lies on the original line, so they effectively fly straight.
- The punch ends when `traveled_distance >= max_distance` or `punch_t >= 1.0`.

### Spin (both modes)
If `spin_rate > 0` (from the JSON), `sprite.rotation` is spun with a smoothstep ease (`ease_in_out()`). The fist slows down at the start and end, and spins fastest mid-punch. Direction depends on `is_left`.

**Caveat for `ThumbsUpFist`:** its `_process()` overwrites `sprite.rotation = fixed_rotation` every frame while punching or retracting. This effectively cancels any spin for that fist.

## 3. Return: `_process_retraction()`

- The fist flies in a **straight line at `return_speed`** toward `original_slot_parent`'s current global position. That parent is still attached to the rotating `FistSlots` on the character, so **the retraction homes in on the character** as it moves.
- `external_speed_multiplier` does not affect the return.
- When the fist is within 10 px, `_reset_fist()` re-parents the fist and slot back to the character and zeroes position and rotation.
- A very high `return_speed` could overshoot the 10 px window in a single frame and jitter. At 300 px/s and 60 fps it moves about 5 px per frame, so it's fine.

### Delayed retraction (bounce stun): `start_retraction_with_delay()`
This is used for shield blocks (0.2 s) and fist clashes (0.3 s). It sets `is_retract_paused`. While paused, `_process_retraction()` only counts down the timer and returns early, so the fist **hangs in place** before returning.

---

## 4. Collisions

The hitbox is only monitored while punching. It is switched off in `_start_retraction()`, so retracting fists can't hit anything.

### Body hits: `_on_hitbox_body_entered()`

**Shields.** A shield is a body in the `"Shields"` group, created in `CharacterBase.spawn_shield_wall()`.
- The punch direction is compared to the shield's facing, `body.global_transform.y`. Because `FistSlots` is rotated `angle - 90°`, local +Y points toward the cursor.
- If `dot < -0.3`, the punch is hitting the front of the shield. The result is:
  - 15 stamina off each of the defender's fists.
  - The defender's dash-cancel window opens (`trigger_dash_cancel_window()`).
  - The attacking fist does a 0.2 s delayed retraction.
- Hits from the side or back pass through to the normal damage logic.

**Targets.** If the body is in `target_group` and has `take_damage()`, it takes damage, a damage number spawns, and the fist retracts (deferred). `take_damage()` also triggers a 0.05 s global hitpause via `get_tree().paused`. Whether fists freeze during it depends on their `process_mode` in the scene, which I can't see.

### Fist vs. fist clashes: `_on_hitbox_area_entered()`
These only count when **both** fists are punching and are on different teams. Each fist runs this logic on itself:

| Weight comparison | Bounces back (0.3 s pause)? | Stamina lost |
|---|---|---|
| This fist heavier | No, keeps going | 10 |
| Equal | Yes | 15 |
| This fist lighter | Yes | 20 |

Equal-weight clashes bounce both fists. A heavier fist plows through and can still reach the target.

## 5. Things that modify the physics

- **Speed boosts.** `FistBase.apply_temporary_speed_boost()` sets `external_speed_multiplier`, which affects both punch modes. It is reset in `_start_retraction()` and `_reset_fist()`.
- **`ThumbsUpFist` overrides the boost.** It changes `move_speed` directly and adds 10 damage, then resets both on a timer. Because the change lives on `move_speed`, retraction does **not** reset it. If you punch again within `synergy_boost_duration`, the next punch may still be boosted.
- **Stamina and disabling.** A disabled fist can't punch. It also slows your movement (`handle_movement_input()`: ×0.5 for one disabled fist, ×0.1 for two). Any fist punching or retracting halves move speed.

## 6. The chain (visual only)

`scripts/ChainDrawer.gd` runs a Verlet rope between the shoulder slot and the fist slot. It calls `get_chain_tension_ratio()` on the end point. As the fist extends, gravity and sag fade toward 0, so the chain pulls taut. It has no effect on fist movement.

## Depends on scene files I can't see

- **Hitbox `collision_mask`.** The shield wall is on layer 2 (`1 << 1`). Each fist's `Hitbox` mask must include layer 2 to detect shields, plus whichever layers the enemies and the other fists' hitboxes are on.
- **`FistStats.json` values** (`arc_strength`, `spin_rate`, speeds, weight). These decide which movement mode each fist uses.
- **`process_mode`** on fists, which determines whether they freeze during hitpause.
